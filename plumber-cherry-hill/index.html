Four problems that cannot wait in Cherry Hill

Some plumbing trouble can sit until Monday. These four cannot, because each one damages the house a little more every hour.

Water escaping a pipe

A split supply line or a failed joint can empty a lot of water into a ceiling or crawl space. Close the main valve, open one low faucet to drain the lines, and call. Our burst pipe repair work cuts out the damaged section and ties in new pipe.

A water heater leaking at the base

A tank that weeps from the bottom seam is finished. Turn off its power or gas control and close the cold supply valve above it. If the tank itself is leaking, water heater replacement is the usual answer.

Sewage or water rising in a drain

When a tub or floor drain gurgles or backs up, stop flushing and stop running water. Avoid contact with anything that has come up from the drain. Tell us what is backing up and where, and ask your sewer utility whether the street main is clear.

A ceiling stain that keeps growing

A wet spot spreading under a bathroom or kitchen points to a pressurized line. Shut the main, keep people out of the room, and stay clear of wet light fixtures. We trace the source with leak detection before anyone opens drywall.

What to do before the plumber arrives

Find the main shutoff

In most houses it sits where the water line enters, often near the meter in a basement or utility closet. Turn it clockwise until it stops. If it will not move, do not force it, and tell the dispatcher.

Cut power and fuel where it is wet

Switch off a leaking water heater's power or gas control. Do not touch a breaker with wet hands or while standing in water. If water is near the panel, stay back and say so on the call.

Clear the floor and take photos

Move rugs, boxes and electronics out of the wet area. Photograph the damage and the valve you closed for any insurance claim.
