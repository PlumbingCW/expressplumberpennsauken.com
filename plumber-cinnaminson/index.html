When a plumbing problem becomes an emergency in Cinnaminson

Sewage backing into the lowest fixture

Stop flushing and avoid the water. A clog in the lateral behaves differently from one in the street line, and the township's sewerage authority handles the public side. For the private side, we use cable or jetting, and our drain cleaning service covers both.

A water line leaking toward the house

Soggy ground by the foundation, a meter that will not stop or sudden low pressure all point to the service line. Shut the main. Leaks between the street and the house are repaired through water line repair when the pipe is on the homeowner's side.

A water heater failing in the night

Water at the base or a relief valve discharging means turning off the fuel or power and closing the cold supply. If you smell gas, leave the house and call the gas utility or 911 first.

Cinnaminson water and sewer: two different agencies

Cinnaminson water comes from New Jersey American Water. In 2021 the utility replaced about 4,530 feet of cast iron mains installed in the 1960s on streets including Heritage Road and Hilltop Road. Houses of that decade often have plumbing of the same vintage, so low pressure after street work is worth a call.

Sewer service is a separate body. The Cinnaminson Sewerage Authority has its office at 1621 Riverton Road, 856-829-5287, and its plant is at 400 N. Randolph Street, 856-829-2271. Ask the authority whether a backup is in the street main or in your own line, then call us for the private side.
