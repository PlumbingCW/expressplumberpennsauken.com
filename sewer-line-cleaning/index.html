Sewage coming up in the house: what it means

A sewer backup does not start at the toilet. Wastewater looks for the lowest opening it can find, so the basement floor drain, a laundry standpipe, or a shower on the lowest level usually shows trouble first. If you flush or run water upstairs and hear gurgling downstairs, the main line is struggling to move the flow.

Gurgling in several drains

Air displaced by a blockage bubbles up through traps in fixtures far from the actual clog.

Floor drain or tub fills first

The lowest fixture on the line is the relief point for backed-up water.

Toilet rises when something else drains

Water has nowhere to go and is pushing back through the closest open path.

Slow everywhere, not just one sink

Each branch joins the main. When the main is partly blocked, all of them slow down together.

Health caution: treat sewage as contaminated

Avoid direct contact with sewage and keep children and pets away from the area. Wear gloves and boots if you must go near it, wash hands thoroughly after, and do not mix cleaning chemicals. Porous items soaked by sewage, such as carpet padding, are often not worth keeping.

Stop the water

Locate the cleanout

It is usually a capped pipe in the basement near the foundation wall or a riser outside, often with a square or round cap. Do not open a cap under pressure, since sewage can rush out.

Keep the area clear

Move belongings off the floor, keep the space ventilated if safe, and stay away from wet outlets and equipment.

Who handles the problem in Pennsauken, NJ: you or the Authority

Pennsauken sewage is collected by the Pennsauken Sewerage Authority, and the Camden County Municipal Utilities Authority does the treatment. Your side of the line runs from the house to where it joins the public main, so a plug in the lateral is yours to clear. When neighbors report the same backup at once, call the Authority and ask about the main before paying for any digging.

Emergency main sewer line cleaning with a cable and camera

Main line cleaning is usually done through the cleanout with a larger power cable and a cutter head matched to the pipe. After the cut we run a camera to see what caused the stoppage, such as roots, grease, a bellied pipe, or a break. The recorded view is what tells you whether to expect another backup next season.

If cutting does not restore full flow, or the camera shows a heavy grease coat, we move to hydro jetting, which cleans the whole pipe wall. A defect found on camera leads to the options on our sewer line repair page.

Sewer drain cleaning versus a house drain clog

The phrase sewer drain cleaning covers anything below the building drain, where a toilet, bath and kitchen line all combine. A single sink clog is a fixture problem and is handled through drain cleaning. When more than one fixture shows trouble, treat it as a sewer line issue.

Septic tanks: rare in Pennsauken

Most Pennsauken addresses are on public sewer, so a septic tank is uncommon here. If you are not sure, check your sewer bill or ask the Pennsauken Sewerage Authority. Pumping and septic field work is a different trade, and we will tell you plainly if the property turns out not to be on sewer.
