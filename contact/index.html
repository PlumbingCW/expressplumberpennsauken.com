What to tell the call taker

A clear description saves time and lets us decide how urgent the job is. You do not need plumbing words, so say what you see, hear, or smell.

Start with the address and ZIP code, because that tells us whether you are in Pennsauken or a nearby town.

  • What is happening: a leak, a backup, no hot water, a toilet that will not stop running, or something else.

  • Whether you have closed the main water valve, and whether it worked.
  • The street address and ZIP code, and whether it is a house, apartment, or business.
  • Any gas smell. If you smell gas, tell us only after you are outside and safe.

What to do while you wait

Safe steps before a plumber arrives

These are general steps. The call taker will adjust them to your situation.

  • Close the main water shut-off if water is flowing where it should not be.
  • Stop using sinks, tubs, showers, and toilets if drains are backing up.
  • Keep people and pets away from standing water near outlets, appliances, or the panel.
  • Turn off power at the breaker only if you can reach it while standing on a dry surface. Otherwise leave it and call.
  • If a water heater is discharging hot water or steam, turn off its power or gas fuel and close the cold supply to it.
  • Avoid contact with sewage, and wash hands thoroughly if you touch it.
  • Move valuables and rugs out of the wet area if it is safe to do so.
  • Take photos of the damage for your records.

What happens after you call

Triage on the phone

The call taker confirms the address, judges how urgent the problem is, and gives safety steps.

A plumber is scheduled

We offer same-day appointments when available. You will be told what to expect when the schedule is set, and we do not promise a fixed arrival time.

On-site look and a price

The plumber examines the problem and explains it. You receive a clear price before work starts and decide whether to go ahead.

Call someone else first in these cases

Some emergencies start outside your property line, and a plumber cannot fix them. A main break in the street or a problem on the utility side of the meter belongs to the water utility.

Most Pennsauken addresses are served by the Merchantville Pennsauken Water Commission (MPWC), whose water emergency line is (856) 663-0044. A few addresses may be on New Jersey American Water, so check your bill to see which company serves you.

Gas is different. If you smell gas, leave the building, do not use switches or phones inside, and call 911 or your gas utility from outside. Call us only after the danger is cleared. Do not wait on a plumber for a gas problem.

Unsure whether it is your pipe or the street?

If water is bubbling up in the road or the lawn at the curb, or the meter box is flooded, the utility may need to act first. If the water is inside your walls or after the meter, it is a plumbing job. Call us either way and we will help sort it out.
